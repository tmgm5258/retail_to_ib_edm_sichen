✅Re-Enrollment Mechanics — Confirmed Approach (Sichen)
QHow does the 30-day re-enrollment loop actually work in HubSpot?
⌄
Confirmed by Sichen: use a 30-day duration step at the end of the workflow.

After E5 sends, the workflow includes a 30-day delay action. The contact stays inside the workflow during this delay — they can't be re-enrolled while already in. Once the 30 days elapse and the workflow ends, if the contact still matches the Active List enrollment trigger (and "Allow re-enrollment" is enabled), HubSpot re-enrolls them and they start a fresh cycle from E1.

Sichen's words: "Re-enroll means they have to exit to rejoin, they can't enter the workflow when they already in the workflow."
QWhy a duration step instead of a date property like last_send_date?
⌄
We initially considered adding a date property (e.g. last_send_date) into the Active List criteria, but Sichen confirmed this isn't how TMGM's HubSpot workflows handle cool-downs.

Sichen's words: "No, there're no last_send_date in criteria. Usually we use duration, adding 30 days for them to exit."

The duration step is HubSpot-native, doesn't require a new property, and is simpler to maintain. Going with this approach.
QWhat needs to be configured in HubSpot for this to work?
⌄
Two things on the workflow itself:

1. 30-day Delay (duration) action placed after E5, before the workflow ends.
2. "Allow re-enrollment" toggled on on the enrollment trigger, so contacts who still match the Active List after the workflow ends get re-enrolled into a fresh cycle.

No custom date property, no Active List criteria changes. Worth a test contact through the full cycle during build to verify behavior before production launch.

⚠️Edge Cases & Gotchas
QWhat if a contact unsubscribes mid-cycle, then re-subscribes weeks later?
⌄
During unsubscribed period: Path 3 exit (suppressed). HubSpot stops sending; contact removed from Active List membership effects.

On re-subscribe: Contact rejoins the Active List → enrollment trigger fires → workflow re-enrolls from E1. Treated as a fresh cycle, not a resumption.
QWhat if a contact's trading account is closed and reopened?
⌄
Depends on Nick's pipeline behavior — confirmed by Nick: ta_first_deposit_time doesn't change when a client closes and re-opens. So Filter 3's 7-day buffer stays anchored to the original FTD. A returning client doesn't get a fresh 7-day wait — they re-enter on the next cycle after the 30-day cool-down (assuming they still match all 8 filters and aren't already an IB).
QWhat if a contact's country changes to restricted, then back?
⌄
During restriction: Path 3 exit (suppressed). Workflow unenrolls; no further sends.

On country reverting to an allowed jurisdiction: Active List criteria matches again → enrollment trigger fires → contact re-enters from E1. Audit log preserves both events for compliance.
QWhat if a contact converts to IB then somehow "unconverts" (portal - user_type reverts away from "IB")?
⌄
Edge case — unlikely in practice but worth flagging.

If portal - user_type reverts away from "IB" / "IB + Client", the contact technically re-matches all 8 entry filters and could re-enter the Retail-to-IB workflow. Recommendation: add an additional Active List criterion "portal - user_type has never been any of IB or IB + Client" (using HubSpot's "has ever been" operator) so a former IB doesn't get re-pitched. Worth a brief conversation with Compliance.
QCan HubSpot accidentally enroll a contact into the workflow twice at the same time?
⌄
No. HubSpot has a built-in safeguard: a contact can only be enrolled in one instance of a given workflow at a time. If they're currently enrolled (or in cool-down), HubSpot will not double-enroll them — even if they re-trigger the enrollment criteria.
